The Challenge

Modern operational environments impose increasingly severe constraints on tactical activities. Operational units in areas without cellular coverage, under jamming, or in GPS-denied conditions face critical gaps in coordination and situational awareness.

Existing solutions require dedicated, visible devices, making discreet deployment difficult and increasing the risk of compromise.

No Infrastructure Required

TheSolution

CERBERUS is a tactical localization and communication system based on a LoRa interconnected devices forming a mesh network.

This technology utilizes a LoRa mesh network integrated into a small device, allowing users to communicate without relying on cellular towers, satellites, or GPS.

The system prioritizes security by employing AES-256-GCM encryption and operating on frequencies that typically bypass standard electronic jamming.

Command and Control interacts through a dedicated Android application on a hardened tablet, providing real-time position tracking, situational awareness via offline maps, and integrated distress signaling.

The mesh topology ensures redundancy and increasing coverage with each additional node, eliminating single points of failure.

This solution addresses the need for reliable coordination in hostile environments where traditional infrastructure is unavailable or compromised.

  • LoRa Mesh Network
  • AES-256-GCM Encryption
  • No Cellular, Satellite or GPS Dependency
  • Hardened Android Tablet for C&C
  • Offline Maps & Situational Awareness
  • Integrated Distress Signaling
