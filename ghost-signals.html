The Challenge

Modern operational environments impose increasingly severe constraints on tactical communications. Operational units in areas without cellular coverage, under jamming, or in GPS-denied conditions face critical gaps in coordination and situational awareness.

Existing solutions require dedicated, visible devices, making discreet deployment difficult and increasing the risk of compromise.

No Infrastructure, No Visible Devices

Encrypted communications over a LoRa mesh network, fully integrated into the smartphone case.

Operational Without Coverage or GPS

Operational even without cellular coverage or GPS.

Jamming Resistant

The system operates on frequencies generally excluded from standard jamming systems.

Discreet Tactical Communications

TheSolution

Ghost Signals is a tactical communication system based on a LoRa mesh network, developed entirely in Italy and integrated into a smartphone case.

The system operates on frequencies generally excluded from standard jamming systems.

Every communication is protected by AES-256-GCM encryption with data integrity authentication.

The mesh topology ensures redundancy and increasing coverage with each additional node, without any single point of failure.

  • LoRa Mesh Network
  • Integrated into the Smartphone Case
  • AES-256-GCM Encryption
  • Data Integrity Authentication
  • Jamming-Resistant Frequencies
  • No Single Point of Failure
