Our Story

Quality and Dependability in Defense aerospace

ITRAD Aerospace & Defense, located in Cascina, close to the metropolitan area of Pisa, stands for quality and dependability in domestic Defense Aerospace applications.

Over the years, we have expanded in the market by offering tailored logistic support services to clients during the process of supplying equipment, devices, various spare parts, as well as the relevant tools and fixtures used in repair and overhaul.

Solid relationships with several key platform manufacturers, combined with customer care and financial strength, currently make Italiana Trading Aerospace & Defense a dependable source for domestic Defense Aerospace OEMs.

For years, Italiana Trading Aerospace & Defense has assisted offshore manufacturers by acting as their exclusive distributor — bridging world-class international brands with Italian and European defense operators.
