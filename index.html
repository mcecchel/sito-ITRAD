Who We Are

Italian Roots, Global Reach.

our story ›

ITRAD Aerospace & Defense, located in Cascina, close to the metropolitan area of Pisa, stands for quality and dependability in domestic Defense Aerospace applications.

Over the years, we have expanded by offering tailored logistic support services to clients during the supply of equipment, devices, spare parts, tools, and fixtures used in repair and overhaul. Solid relationships with key platform manufacturers, combined with financial strength, make ITRAD a dependable source for domestic Defense Aerospace OEMs.
