OPS-CORE, a Gentex company, is the world’s leading manufacturer of ballistic helmets and tactical head protection systems for military, law enforcement, and special operations forces. ITRAD holds confirmed exclusive distributorship status as listed on the official Gentex global distributors page.

Ops-Core is the exclusive provider of USSOCOM’s FAST Tactical Headborne System (FTHS), the standard issue helmet platform for U.S. Special Operations Command. Gentex Corporation, headquartered in Carbondale, Pennsylvania, draws on a 130-year legacy of protective equipment manufacturing. The Integrated Modular Design and Open Architecture of OPS-CORE helmet systems allow configuration for any mission, with seamless integration of optics, communications, and respiratory protection accessories.

DIODON Drone
Technology

Founded in 2017 in Toulouse, France, DIODON Drone Technology designs and manufactures the world’s first inflatable, fully waterproof micro-UAV — purpose-built for ISR missions in maritime, coastal, and littoral environments where conventional drones cannot operate.

The flagship HP30 is engineered to survive water contact, float, be recovered, and redeployed — rather than treating immersion as mission-ending. Deployable from boats as small as 3 metres, it supports day/night reconnaissance, surveillance, and tactical operations for navies, coastal security forces, and special operations units.

Authorized Distributor

Ametrine

Ametrine is the industry leader in Multi-Spectral Signature Management (MSSM) technology — engineering camouflage and concealment solutions that operate across the visible light spectrum and beyond. Their systems empower operators to move unseen and with full mobility on the modern battlefield.

Founded in 2011 in Israel, Ametrine was born from its CTO’s own combat experience with thermal-sensor detection during the Second Lebanon War. Rather than blocking emitted radiation, its technology lets operators “blend” into the environment, and the company now serves the U.S. Department of Defense through an American subsidiary.

ITRAD distributes Ametrine’s MSSM equipment to qualified end-users across Europe and designated regions, in full compliance with applicable export regulations.

Exclusive Distributor

REBS

REBS by H. Henriksen is a Norwegian specialist in tactical boarding and breaching systems for military and special operations forces. ITRAD distributes REBS products to defense operators in authorized markets, targeting demanding tactical environments requiring extreme reliability.

The REBS (Rapid Entering and Boarding Systems) line was established in 2003 in response to a Norwegian Defense initiative for safer, more effective boarding operations at sea. Built from carbon fibre and titanium for maximum strength-to-weight ratio, the range spans ladders, hooks, telescopic poles, and launchers used by naval, military, and special operations forces worldwide.
