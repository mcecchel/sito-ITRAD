Military Aviation

Defense Aviation

Exclusive Italian distributor for military avionics test, ground-support and mission-support equipment. From IFF transponder test sets to aircraft weighing kits and pitot-static accessory kits, ITRAD serves Air Force maintenance units, MRO facilities, and NATO allies with full certification and export compliance.

  • Tel-Instrument Electronics — IFF & transponder test sets
  • Barfield Inc. — air data, GSTE and pitot-static testers
  • Nav-Aids Ltd. — pitot-static test adaptor kits
  • VPG Onboard Weighing — aircraft weighing systems
