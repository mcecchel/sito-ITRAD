01 AS9120 — Rev B

Aerospace Quality Management

SAE International / IAQG

AS9120B Certificate ›

AS9120 is the aerospace industry’s dedicated quality management standard for stockist distributors of aerospace parts, materials, and assemblies. Our certification to AS9120 Rev B ensures that every item we source, handle, and deliver meets the highest levels of traceability, documentation integrity, and quality control.

  • Full traceability from OEM to end-user
  • Counterfeit parts prevention program
  • Documented quality management system
  • Regular surveillance audits by accredited body
  • Supplier qualification and monitoring
  • Non-conformance management

Additional Compliance

Regulatory &
Compliance Framework

Export Administration Regulations

Compliance with U.S. Department of Commerce Export Administration Regulations for dual-use items on the Commerce Control List (CCL), including license determination and EEI filing.

Counterfeit Parts Prevention

ITRAD Aerospace & Defense operates a rigorous counterfeit avoidance program aligned with AS5553 and AS6174, including supplier qualification, receiving inspection, and reporting procedures.

End-User Compliance

All transactions involving controlled goods include thorough end-user verification, collection of End-User Certificates (EUCs), and post-shipment validation as required.
