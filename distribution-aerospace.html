Tel-Instrument
Electronics Corp.

Est. 1947

Tel-Instrument Electronics Corp. is the industry leader in developing and producing field-tested, rugged avionic flight line and bench test sets for demanding military and commercial customers.

Founded in 1947, and has been providing avionic ramp and bench testers to both commercial and military customers for the last 35 years. Tel-Instruments historical design emphasis has been to provide the customer with user-friendly, rugged, reliable, and affordable units.

Tel’s products have led the avionics support equipment industry to higher levels of integration by combining more test functions into a single unit, and thereby reducing customer acquisition, training, and life-cycle support costs. Tel-Instrument is strongly committed to taking lifetime responsibility for product design, function, and reliability, for depot-level support, and for configuration management.

Headquartered in East Rutherford, New Jersey, and publicly listed on the NYSE, Tel-Instrument is a key supplier of encrypted Mode 5 IFF (Identification Friend or Foe) test technology, the standard the U.S. and NATO countries are migrating to for secure identification systems.

Nav-Aids Ltd.

Est. 1962

For more than 55 years, Nav-Aids Ltd. of Montreal, Quebec, Canada, has specialized in pitot-static test adaptors and air data accessories kits for every type of aircraft: general aviation, commuter, corporate, transport and military to missile defense, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV) and space vehicles.

More than 500 different types of aircraft
We design, assemble and test our products in a state-of-the-art facility located near Montreal's Trudeau International Airport.

Our adaptors are available as stand-alone items or in aircraft-specific kits that include all required adaptors, hose assemblies, pre-test tools, control consoles (LSUs), spare seals, lubricating fluid, hose suspension fixtures and more, all contained in transit cases equipped with dunnage to properly store and protect contents.

Nav-Aids is ISO 9001 certified and remains a family-owned company. Its military credentials include a U.S. Department of Defense contract for pressure test adapters used by U.S. Army maintenance crews on Black Hawk helicopters.

Barfield

Est. 1945

Founded in 1945, Barfield has six facilities located near airports in Miami (MIA), Phoenix (PHX), Louisville (SDF), Atlanta (ATL) and Doral (MIA), to satisfy the needs of customers operating commercial or regional aircraft in North, Central and South America.

Barfield’s support services are organized into three main activities: Adaptive Services, Distribution, and Ground Support Test Equipment (GSTE). Barfield is an authorized repair facility for more than 30 European and U.S. OEMs. Through its Airline Programs division, Barfield supplies complete customized support programs for Airbus, Boeing, Regional Jet and Turboprop aircraft, as well as helicopter operators.

Barfield is also a GSTE manufacturer of Air Data/Pitot Static testers, RVSM Air Data testers, APU testers, Cable Tensiometer testers, Fuel Quantity testers, Pressure testers, Nav/Comm Transponder test sets, Pitot/Static Adapters and Turbine Temperature testers.

Barfield is part of the Air France Industries KLM Engineering & Maintenance (AFI KLM E&M) network, one of the world’s largest MRO providers, and is a FAA and EASA Certified Repair Station. In 2025, the company marked its 80th anniversary in business.

VPG Onboard
Weighing

VPG Onboard Weighing is part of Vishay Precision Group (NYSE: VPG), a global specialist in resistive foil technology, sensors, and sensor-based systems for stress, force, weight, and pressure measurement. Headquartered in Bradford, UK, the brand traces its roots to the 1980s as PM Onboard — the UK’s longest-established manufacturer of on-vehicle weighing systems — becoming part of Vishay Precision Group in 2010.

Built around solid-state strain-gauge and load-cell sensors with no moving parts, VPG’s weighing technology spans from commercial vehicles to aerospace platforms through the JetWeigh® JackPoint Aircraft Weighing System family. The group’s aerospace weighing technology delivers NATO-stocked precision weighing solutions for C130J, C27J, and ATR72 platforms.

VPG is ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949:2016 certified. With sales offices across North America, Europe and Asia, VPG operates an extensive and growing network of channel partners delivering its weighing expertise to a global market.
